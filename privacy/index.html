Privacy policy

Your information and this website.

How this website handles enquiries, external services and the information you choose to share.

Perwira Visa · Indonesia
Current form status: the enquiry form prepares a message locally in your browser and opens WhatsApp with that message. You review it and tap Send in WhatsApp. This website has no form-processing backend.

Information entered in the form

The form asks for your name, a way to contact you, the type of assistance and a description of your plans. Additional optional questions depend on the selected service.

The form values are held in the current page. The application does not save them to local storage or session storage. Reloading or leaving the page clears the page's current form state, subject to any separate autofill behavior provided by your browser.

Opening WhatsApp

When you choose Open WhatsApp, your form answers are included in a WhatsApp link so that WhatsApp can prepare your message. This shares the prepared text with WhatsApp; you still choose whether to send it to Perwira Visa. WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply.

Perwira Visa uses the messages you send to understand your enquiry, reply and discuss the relevant assistance. Messages and any subsequent service records are handled through the communication services and business arrangements used for your enquiry. Contact us to ask about access, correction, deletion or retention of your information.

Copying your message

If you choose Copy message, the text is copied to your device's clipboard. Clipboard history, autofill and any later sharing are controlled by your device, browser and the services you choose to use.

Documents and credentials

The form does not accept file uploads. Do not enter passport numbers or scans, complete payment details, bank credentials, passwords, private keys or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Analytics and external content

This website does not include application analytics or marketing trackers. Images, styling and scripts are served as local website assets. The hosting service may process technical connection information needed to deliver and protect the site.

The About page embeds Google Maps. When the map loads, your browser connects to Google, which may receive connection information and use cookies according to its own privacy settings and terms. WhatsApp links connect to WhatsApp when you choose to use them.

Links to government sources and photo credits take you to external websites, whose own privacy terms apply.
