Terms & website use

Understand the information and service scope.

These statements explain use of the website. The terms for a service engagement should be agreed separately for the actual work.

Perwira Visa · Indonesia

General information

The guides explain common immigration, employment, company, property and administrative questions. They do not decide individual eligibility and should not be treated as a substitute for appropriately qualified advice on a specific matter.

Current requirements

Requirements can change and can depend on the activity, documents, location and circumstances. Refer to current official sources and obtain the necessary review before submitting an application, starting work, making an investment or committing to a property transaction.

Engaging Perwira Visa

The work, fees, responsibilities, deliverables and any additional professional involvement should be agreed before services begin. Reading this website or preparing an enquiry does not itself create an accepted engagement or confirm an outcome.

How services are delivered

Accounting and business-administration support are direct Perwira Visa services within the agreed scope. Website and digital-development work is performed by an appropriate third-party provider; Perwira Visa helps clarify requirements, make introductions and coordinate where appropriate.

The commercial arrangement for any partner work should be confirmed for the particular engagement. This page does not assume who contracts with whom or waive responsibility for all third-party actions.

Decisions and outcomes

Government decisions remain with the relevant authorities. The website does not promise approval of every application, investment returns, a risk-free property transaction, search rankings or guaranteed business results.

Digital assets

For a website project, ownership and appropriate account access should be arranged clearly for the client. Applicable licenses continue to govern third-party software, images, fonts and other licensed materials.

External sources

Official reference links are provided to help readers check information. Their inclusion does not imply government endorsement of Perwira Visa.

Enquiries through WhatsApp

The contact form opens WhatsApp with a prepared message. You review and send the message in WhatsApp. Opening or sending an enquiry does not confirm an accepted engagement, a fee or an outcome.
