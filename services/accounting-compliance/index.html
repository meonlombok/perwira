The short answer

Reliable records help owners understand cash, income, expenses and outstanding balances. Agree the bookkeeping, reporting and administrative responsibilities clearly—including any separate specialist tax work.

Support built around the way your business operates

Bookkeeping

Organize transactions and supporting documents, categorize financial activity and reconcile records.

Management information

Prepare the agreed reports on income, expenses, cash and outstanding balances.

Catch-up work

Assess missing periods and records, then agree how to bring the accounts up to date.

Recurring administration

Keep agreed reporting and business-administration tasks visible, with clear responsibilities and dates.

Make the boundary with tax work clear

Bookkeeping and tax administration are connected, but they are not identical. Your scope should identify which work covers records, calculations, filings, advice or representation—and who is responsible for each.

Perwira Visa's accounting staff provide the agreed accounting support directly. Where specialist work requires a qualified professional, that role should be confirmed separately. Management reports should not be presented as an independent audit.

Indonesian corporate taxpayers are generally required to maintain bookkeeping. The specific tax position and filing obligations should be checked for the actual business.

A practical monthly rhythm

  1. Collect and organize

    Bring sales, expenses, bank activity and supporting documents into an agreed process.

  2. Reconcile and clarify

    Compare records, identify missing information and resolve questions with the business.

  3. Report and follow up

    Prepare the agreed reports and highlight the next administrative tasks.

What to have ready

Keep personal and company transactions clearly separated. For the initial enquiry, describe what is available; do not upload bank statements or credentials into the general form.

  • The company details and the periods needing support.
  • Approximate monthly transaction volume and current record-keeping method.
  • Sales and purchase records, supporting invoices and receipts.
  • Bank and payment account information, provided through an agreed method.
  • Payroll requirements and existing reports, where relevant.

Questions & answers

Does Perwira Visa handle accounting directly?

Yes. Accounting and business-administration support are delivered by Perwira Visa's own staff within the agreed scope. Specialist work that requires a particular professional qualification should be identified separately before the engagement begins.

What does bookkeeping support cover?

It can include organizing transaction records, categorizing income and expenses, reconciling bank activity and maintaining supporting documents. The agreed scope should state the reporting period, transaction volume, information you must supply and the reports to be delivered.

Is accounting the same as tax advice?

No. Accounting records the business's financial activity. Tax work applies the relevant rules to that activity. A scope of work should distinguish bookkeeping, tax administration, specialist advice and representation, and identify who is responsible for each.

Can you help if my records are behind?

Yes. Start by identifying the periods involved and gathering bank statements, sales records, invoices and expense evidence. The team can then assess missing information and agree a practical catch-up scope before routine monthly work begins.

Can payroll be included?

Payroll administration may be included in the agreed scope. Confirm the employee information, reporting responsibilities, approval process and any tax or employment-related work separately so that each task has a clear owner.

What reports will the business receive?

Agree the reports and delivery schedule before work starts. Depending on the engagement, these can include information on income, expenses, cash, receivables and payables. Management reporting should not be described as an independent audit unless that separate service is actually engaged.
