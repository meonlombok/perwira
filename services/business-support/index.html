The short answer

Perwira Visa does not develop websites internally. We help clarify the requirement, introduce an appropriate professional provider and coordinate the wider business context where useful. The provider performs the technical work.

What the development partner can support

The final deliverables, fees, timeline and support arrangement are agreed for the individual project. Search rankings and commercial results are not guaranteed.

One clear process

  1. Explain the business need

    Tell us what the business does and whether the project is a new website, redesign, migration or ongoing support.

  2. Clarify and introduce

    Perwira Visa helps define the requirement and connects you with a suitable professional development partner.

  3. Agree delivery and access

    The provider scopes the technical work. Confirm the commercial arrangement, responsibilities, handover and ongoing support before work begins.

Your business should keep control of its assets

Arrange the domain, hosting, website administration and important digital accounts so that the client retains appropriate ownership and access. Developers should receive the permissions needed for their work rather than becoming the sole permanent gatekeeper.

  • Confirm the domain registrant and account owner.
  • Retain appropriate access to hosting and website administration.
  • Agree access to analytics, search accounts and backup copies.
  • Document the handover and how access is removed when work ends.
  • Identify third-party software, subscriptions and licenses.
Ownership of your website and accounts does not override the license terms of third-party software, fonts or stock media. Have these items listed in the project scope.

Already have a website? Start with what exists.

Give the provider the website address and describe the problem: an outdated design, difficult updates, hosting issues, poor mobile experience or a required migration.

A review should identify what is worth keeping, which dependencies matter and how content and links will be handled. You do not have to decide the technology before explaining the business need.

A useful first brief

  • Business name, audience and services.
  • Existing website address, if any.
  • The main problem or desired outcome.
  • The required scope and preferred timing.
  • Who will supply content and manage future updates.
Do not send passwords, registrar credentials, hosting logins or private access keys through the enquiry form.

Questions & answers

Does Perwira Visa build the website itself?

No. Perwira Visa helps clarify the requirement and introduces an appropriate third-party web-development professional. The professional provider scopes and performs the technical work. Perwira Visa may coordinate the wider business context where appropriate.

What can the development partner help with?

The confirmed scope can include a new website, domain and hosting registration, migration, maintenance, backups, analytics, basic SEO, updates and redesign. Availability, deliverables and ongoing support are agreed with the provider for the specific project.

Who controls the domain, hosting and website?

The arrangement should preserve the client's ownership and appropriate administrative access to important digital assets and accounts. Agree the domain registrant, account ownership, handover and backup access before work starts. Third-party software and other licensed materials remain subject to their applicable licenses.

Can an existing website be migrated or redesigned?

Yes, where technically feasible. The provider should first review the website, hosting, domain configuration and dependencies. The scope should identify what is kept, what is changed and how redirects, content and essential access will be handled.

Are maintenance and backups included automatically?

They should be agreed explicitly. Confirm which updates are covered, who monitors backups, how a restore is requested and what support period applies. A website-development project does not automatically include indefinite ongoing maintenance.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No. Basic SEO can cover clear page titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, sitemap and crawlability. Rankings, traffic and enquiries depend on many factors and should not be promised as guaranteed outcomes.

Should I send hosting or domain passwords in the enquiry?

No. Describe the current setup without sending passwords or account credentials. If access is needed later, agree a suitable access method with the development professional and provide only the permissions required for the work.
