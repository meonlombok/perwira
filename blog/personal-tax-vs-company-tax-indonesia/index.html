Personal Tax vs Company Tax in Indonesia: What Foreign Business Owners Need to Understand

Understand how personal tax and PT PMA company tax differ in Indonesia, including residence, salary, dividends, loans, reimbursements and returns.

Your PT PMA files its tax return and pays its tax. You own the shares, manage the business and receive money from its bank account. Does that mean your Indonesian tax obligations are complete?

Not necessarily. The company and you are separate taxpayers. Company tax concerns the company’s income. Personal tax concerns income belonging to you, subject to your residence, the type of payment and applicable exemptions.

Personal tax vs company tax in Indonesia

Consider a foreign investor, Alex, who owns and manages a PT PMA operating accommodation in Lombok. Guests contract with the company, the company employs staff, and Alex receives remuneration.

The accommodation revenue belongs to the company. Alex’s remuneration belongs to Alex. A later dividend is another transaction with its own tax treatment.

A company’s corporate income tax is not normally a credit against its shareholder’s personal income tax. Payroll tax withheld specifically for the individual is different: it may count toward that individual’s annual liability.

First establish your personal tax residence

Indonesian tax residence is not determined solely by nationality, share ownership or having a KITAS.

Under the domestic rules, an individual can become resident by:

  • Residing in Indonesia.
  • Being present for more than 183 days within a 12-month period.
  • Being in Indonesia during a tax year with an intention to reside there.

The days need not be consecutive, and part of a day counts as a day. PER-23/PJ/2025 provides current guidance on determining resident and nonresident status. [1]

Consequently, “I have not reached 184 days” is not a complete defence. A long-term home, work arrangement or other evidence of settlement may matter independently.

For Alex, moving to Lombok to manage the business and establishing a home requires a residence assessment from the outset. Keep travel records, residence documents and relevant contracts.

Residence affects the scope of taxable income

Indonesian-resident individuals are generally taxed on worldwide income, subject to exemptions and treaty provisions. Nonresidents generally face Indonesian tax on Indonesian-source income under the relevant rules.

There is a limited four-tax-year regime for qualifying foreign nationals with specified expertise. It requires eligibility and the prescribed application process; owning a PT PMA or holding an investor stay permit does not automatically qualify someone. Treaty interaction must also be checked. [2]

What tax does the PT PMA pay?

An Indonesian-incorporated PT PMA is generally an Indonesian-resident corporate taxpayer.

The standard corporate income-tax rate is 22% of taxable income, not automatically 22% of revenue. Accounting profit must be adjusted for tax purposes, and applicable facilities or special regimes can change the calculation. [3]

Avoid assuming every small or newly formed company qualifies for a turnover-based final tax. PP 20/2026 changed eligibility for the small-business final-tax regime. Check the current provisions and any transition rules rather than relying on older setup advice. [4]

Company tax includes more than corporate income tax

Depending on its activities, a PT PMA may also have obligations involving:

  • Employee income-tax withholding.
  • Withholding on rent, services, interest or dividends.
  • VAT where applicable.
  • Local taxes relevant to accommodation, food or other activities.

Those payments serve different purposes. Tax withheld from an employee is not the company’s own corporate income tax.

Salary and director remuneration

Salary and director remuneration generally compensate actual work or corporate responsibilities. They should have a documented basis, appropriate approval and consistent payroll treatment.

For the company, reasonable business-related remuneration may be deductible under the applicable rules. For the recipient, it is generally personal income.

Resident individual employment withholding usually falls under Article 21. PMK 168/2023 governs the calculation framework, including effective-rate withholding during ordinary periods and reconciliation in the final period for regular employees. [5]

Personal rates differ from the corporate rate

Resident individuals’ ordinary annual taxable income is subject to progressive bands:

  • 5% on the first Rp60 million.
  • 15% above Rp60 million up to Rp250 million.
  • 25% above Rp250 million up to Rp500 million.
  • 30% above Rp500 million up to Rp5 billion.
  • 35% above Rp5 billion.

These are marginal rates on taxable income after applicable deductions and allowances—not a single rate applied to every rupiah received. [6]

If Alex receives a director’s salary, the company’s payroll withholding and Alex’s personal annual reconciliation must agree. The company paying its own tax does not remove this requirement.

Dividends are different from salary

A dividend distributes profits to shareholders. It is not payment for employment and is not a deductible salary expense for the company.

Before distribution, confirm profits, required reserves and corporate approvals. A payment described as a dividend in the bank reference is not sufficient.

Indonesian-resident individual shareholders

Qualifying domestic dividends can be excluded from personal income tax when the prescribed Indonesian investment and reporting conditions are met. The exemption is conditional.

Where qualifying formal dividends do not meet the investment exemption, resident individuals generally face 10% final tax, with self-payment obligations under the applicable rules. Do not assume the company has handled that payment. [2]

Informal distributions need separate analysis. Finance Ministry guidance in 2026 highlights different withholding treatment for dividends outside a RUPS or lawful interim-dividend process. [7]

Nonresident shareholders

Dividends paid to nonresidents generally attract 20% Article 26 withholding on the gross amount, unless an applicable treaty provides relief. [8]

For illustration, Rp100 million of gross dividends subject to 20% withholding leaves Rp80 million payable to the shareholder. That is separate from tax previously paid on company profits.

The correct dividend tax for a foreigner in Indonesia therefore depends on residence and eligibility, not merely a foreign passport.

Loan repayments and reimbursements: not every transfer is income

Shareholder-loan principal

Suppose Alex previously lent the company Rp600 million under a genuine loan agreement. The company later repays Rp150 million of principal.

That repayment normally reduces the company’s liability and Alex’s loan receivable. It is not automatically salary or a dividend.

Interest is different: it represents income to the lender and may trigger withholding.

Calling a withdrawal “loan repayment” without an actual outstanding loan does not establish this treatment.

Expense reimbursements

If Alex personally pays a supplier Rp8 million for company equipment, a supported reimbursement can settle the amount owed to Alex.

Keep the invoice, proof of payment, business purpose and approval. The company records the equipment or expense and the corresponding payable.

A private holiday or household purchase cannot be converted into a tax-free business reimbursement merely by using that description. Allowances, markups and benefits may have different tax consequences.

Overseas payment does not mean overseas-source income

“If I am paid overseas, Indonesia cannot tax me” is a particularly risky assumption.

The location of the receiving bank account does not determine income source. Work performed in Indonesia may generate Indonesian-source income even when a foreign customer or employer pays abroad.

The special regime for qualifying foreign experts expressly includes remuneration for work, services or activities in Indonesia even if paid outside Indonesia. [2]

Separately, a resident individual may need to report genuinely foreign-source income, such as overseas rental income or investment returns, subject to the relevant rules.

If Alex personally undertakes consulting work from Lombok for overseas customers, first establish whether the contracts belong to Alex or the PT PMA. Customer location alone does not resolve the tax position.

Transferring previously accumulated savings between personal accounts is also different from earning new income. Preserve records explaining the source.

Tax treaties can help, but benefits are not automatic

Treaties may allocate taxing rights, limit withholding or provide relief from double taxation. They do not make all foreign business owners exempt.

PMK 112/2025 governs current treaty-application procedures. Relevant checks include tax residence, documentation, beneficial ownership and anti-abuse provisions. [9]

Foreign tax credits may be available under Indonesian rules, subject to limitations and supporting evidence. Tax paid abroad is not automatically creditable in full, and company tax cannot simply be substituted for personal foreign tax.

Annual personal and corporate returns

Your personal return

Where required, the personal annual return brings together relevant income, withholding credits, final-tax income, exempt income, assets and liabilities.

Prepare:

  • Payroll and withholding certificates.
  • Dividend records and exemption evidence.
  • Overseas income and tax documents.
  • Bank, investment, shareholding and loan balances.
  • Relevant family and tax-residence information.

Having no additional tax to pay does not necessarily mean there is no filing obligation. Confirm your registration and filing status rather than assuming your accountant’s company engagement includes personal returns.

The company’s return

The corporate return is based on company records: financial statements, tax reconciliation, credits, instalments and supporting schedules.

Ordinary annual filing deadlines are three months after year-end for individuals and four months for companies. For calendar-year taxpayers, these normally mean 31 March and 30 April respectively. Check formal extensions or specific relief separately. [10]

DJP’s 2026 penalty relief for certain personal filings did not permanently change the statutory deadline. [11]

Five misunderstandings to avoid

  • “My PT PMA pays tax, so I need no personal return.” These are separate obligations.
  • “Dividends and salary are taxed alike.” Their legal basis and tax treatment differ.
  • “Every company transfer is my income.” Genuine principal repayment and reimbursement require separate classification.
  • “Offshore payment prevents Indonesian tax.” Residence and income source still matter.
  • “Company expenses are my personal deductions.” An expense belongs in the records of the taxpayer entitled to claim it, without double counting.

For Alex’s Lombok business, the practical solution is separate bank accounts, clear payment descriptions and a monthly reconciliation between company records and personal receipts.

FAQ

Does owning a PT PMA automatically make me tax resident?

No. Residence depends on the applicable personal tests and, where relevant, treaty provisions.

Can the same accountant handle both returns?

Yes, but confirm that both engagements are included and that separate records and calculations are maintained.

Must I report an exempt dividend?

Exemption does not necessarily remove reporting obligations. Follow the relevant return and investment-reporting requirements.

What should I check before transferring money to myself?

Establish whether it is remuneration, dividend, reimbursement or genuine debt repayment, then confirm approval, documentation and tax treatment.
