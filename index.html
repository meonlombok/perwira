Does any ITAS allow me to work?

No. An ITAS is granted for a particular purpose. Work activity must fit the immigration permission and any applicable foreign-worker requirements. Before starting, check the employer, role and actual duties together with the permit basis.

What is a PT PMA?

PT PMA is the commonly used term for an Indonesian limited liability company with foreign investment. The company must have an eligible business activity, the appropriate ownership structure and the required registration and licensing. Incorporation and permission to operate are connected but separate steps.

Can a foreign individual hold Hak Milik?

A foreign individual cannot hold Hak Milik, Indonesia's freehold land title. Other rights or contractual arrangements may be available subject to eligibility and the property. Before committing, identify the exact right being offered and who would legally hold it.

Does Perwira Visa build the website itself?

No. Perwira Visa helps clarify the requirement and introduces an appropriate third-party web-development professional. The professional provider scopes and performs the technical work. Perwira Visa may coordinate the wider business context where appropriate.
